The Motoring Journey

Not just a collection.
A life on the road.

Duraimohan's cars have travelled far beyond the garage, appearing at vintage-car shows, heritage displays, rallies, club gatherings and motoring events across South India.

THE BEGINNING · 1996

A Collection Takes Shape

Duraimohan begins building a collection around a simple idea: preserve remarkable automobiles, keep them running and let people experience their history.

SOUTH INDIA · ONGOING

Shows & Displays

DMC cars have been taken to automotive shows and public displays across South India, bringing historic machinery out of private ownership and into the enthusiast community.

KARNATAKA · RALLIES

Heritage on the Road

DMC participation extends to vintage-car rallies and heritage drives in Karnataka, where the cars become moving pieces of automotive history rather than static exhibits.

PUDUCHERRY · BEACH ROAD

The Seafront Display

Classic automobiles have also formed part of public heritage displays along the Puducherry beachfront. In 2020, Autocar India documented Duraimohan's 1964 Volkswagen Beetle participating in the Chennai–Pondy Heritage Drive.

VOLKSWAGEN COMMUNITY

Club Culture

From Volkswagen gatherings to marque-focused communities, DMC has remained connected to the clubs and enthusiasts who keep classic motoring culture alive.

TODAY

The Archive Continues

Every rally, show, display, photograph and story becomes part of a growing DMC archive, ready to be documented alongside the automobiles themselves.
